THE PROBLEM

Millions of passengers. Thousands of cameras. Not enough eyes.

Indian Railways already runs one of the densest CCTV networks in the country — but footage is watched reactively, after an incident, by a handful of control-room staff who cannot monitor hundreds of feeds at once.

13,000+trains run daily across the network
7,325stations, most with legacy CCTV, few with active analytics
1:400+typical ratio of control-room staff to camera feeds
Hoursaverage time to manually locate an incident in archived footage

Why manual monitoring breaks down

A human operator can meaningfully attend to only a handful of screens at once. Across a large station's camera wall, that means most feeds are effectively unwatched — crowd crushes, chain-snatching, trespassing, and safety violations are caught only after a complaint, if at all.

Why this is solvable now

The cameras, cabling, and NVR infrastructure already exist. What's missing is an intelligence layer — models that watch every feed continuously, flag only what matters, and route it to the right person in seconds. ALGORITHM AVENGERS is built to sit on top of the network Indian Railways already owns.

THE SOLUTION

Three problems, one intelligence layer

ALGORITHM AVENGERS processes existing CCTV streams through purpose-built computer vision models, organised around the three mandates in the problem statement.

01 / CROWD MANAGEMENT

Crowd Management

Real-time density estimation across platforms, FOBs, and concourses, with predictive alerts before a crowd becomes a crush.

  • Live headcount & density heatmaps per zone
  • Predictive surge alerts ahead of train arrivals
  • Automatic gate & entry-flow recommendations
  • Historical footfall patterns for resource planning
02 / CRIME PREVENTION

Crime Prevention

Behavioural anomaly detection that flags suspicious activity live, instead of relying on post-incident footage review.

  • Unattended baggage & object-left-behind detection
  • Loitering, trespass & fight/scuffle recognition
  • Instant alert routing to RPF control room
  • Auto-bookmarked evidence clips for FIR filing
03 / WORK MONITORING

Work Monitoring

Objective, automated tracking of cleaning rounds, staff presence, and safety-gear compliance across shifts.

  • Cleaning-schedule adherence per platform/zone
  • PPE & uniform compliance checks
  • Staff attendance & post coverage verification
  • Shift-wise digital compliance reports for supervisors
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

From existing camera to control-room alert

ALGORITHM AVENGERS is designed as a layer that plugs into the CCTV and NVR infrastructure Indian Railways already operates — no camera replacement, minimal new wiring.

01 · INGEST

Existing CCTV / NVR

RTSP streams pulled directly from station NVRs — analog feeds passed through low-cost encoders where needed.

→
02 · EDGE

Edge inference unit

A station-side GPU box runs lightweight detection models (YOLO-class) for low-latency, bandwidth-light processing.

→
03 · CLOUD / CORE

Central analytics engine

Aggregates events across stations, runs heavier models (tracking, re-ID-free crowd counting, pattern prediction).

→
04 · DECISION

Alert & routing layer

Rules engine converts model output into prioritised alerts, routed to RPF, station master, or cleaning supervisor.

→
05 · ACTION

Control room + mobile app

Operators see live dashboards; field staff get push alerts with camera snapshot, location, and suggested action.

TECHNOLOGY

Built on proven, deployable components

Every layer chosen for real-world station conditions: variable bandwidth, legacy hardware, and 24×7 uptime requirements.

COMPUTER VISION

Detection & tracking

YOLOv8/RT-DETR for object & person detection, DeepSORT-style tracking, OpenPose for posture and PPE checks.

CROWD ANALYTICS

Density estimation

CSRNet-style crowd-counting models producing per-zone density maps without identifying individuals.

INFRASTRUCTURE

Edge + cloud hybrid

Jetson-class edge units at stations, Kubernetes-orchestrated inference in the cloud, RTSP/ONVIF ingestion.

APPLICATION LAYER

Dashboard & alerts

React-based control-room UI, mobile app for field staff, rules engine for alert prioritisation and routing.

PRIVACY & ETHICS

Designed with the same care as any surveillance system deserves

The problem statement itself flags privacy and ethical risk — ALGORITHM AVENGERS treats that as a design constraint, not an afterthought.

No facial recognition on the general public

Crowd and behaviour models work on movement, posture, and object detection — not on identifying individual passengers.

Short, policy-bound retention

Raw footage retention follows existing Railway CCTV policy; only flagged event clips are retained longer, with audit logs.

Human-in-the-loop for action

ALGORITHM AVENGERS flags and prioritises — every enforcement action is still taken by an RPF officer or station authority, not the model.

Auditable, explainable alerts

Every alert carries the triggering clip and confidence score, so decisions can be reviewed and models held accountable.

IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

A phased rollout, not a big-bang deployment

PHASE 1 · 0–3 MONTHS

Pilot at one high-footfall station

Deploy on existing CCTV at a single major station (e.g. a metro-city terminus), covering crowd density and unattended-object detection only.

PHASE 2 · 3–8 MONTHS

Expand model coverage

Add work-monitoring and behavioural-anomaly models; integrate alert routing with the RPF control room and station master's office.

PHASE 3 · 8–14 MONTHS

Zonal rollout

Extend to all major stations within a division, with a shared cloud analytics backbone and standardised dashboard.

PHASE 4 · 14+ MONTHS

Pan-India scaling

Progressive rollout across zones, prioritised by footfall and incident history, with continuous model retraining on field data.
